Lower quarter dark and quiet for translucent dialogue overlay. Style: richly painted anime visual-novel background, hand-painted detailed materials, restrained cinematic light, elegant and atmospheric, warm lantern gold against charcoal wood and blue-green dusk, subtle paper grain. No people, no silhouettes, no text, no lettering, no logos, no UI, no foreground furniture obstructing the center. This is environment artwork only; character will be rendered separately.
